Course Overview

About this course

This course teaches foundational generative AI principles, practical prompting techniques, and safe workplace practices across routine office workflows. It is designed for business professionals, administrative staff, and project coordinators who possess basic office application skills but have no prior programming or AI background. After completing the training, learners will be able to draft correspondence, process spreadsheet data, supervise multi-step AI workflows, and maintain organizational confidentiality and compliance standards.
Target Audience
Business professionals, administrative assistants, operations personnel, and project coordinators seeking to integrate generative AI safely into daily office tasks.
Prerequisites
Basic familiarity with web browsers and standard office applications (word processors and spreadsheets). No programming or prior AI experience required.
Learning Outcomes

Course learning objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:

  • Identify core generative AI concepts, capabilities, limitations, and workplace compliance rules concerning data privacy and intellectual property.
  • Construct structured, role-based prompts using proven prompting frameworks across major conversational AI platforms.
  • Generate, summarize, and refine professional business documents and communications while applying effective human-in-the-loop review.
  • Apply AI-assisted prompts to spreadsheet tasks including formula creation, data cleaning, and tabular summary generation.
  • Supervise multi-step, task-oriented AI workflows and simulated agents while mitigating hallucinations and quality risks.
