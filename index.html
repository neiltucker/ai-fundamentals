Course Overview

About this course

This course teaches foundational generative AI principles, practical prompting techniques, and safe workplace practices across routine office workflows. It is designed for business professionals, administrative staff, and project coordinators who possess basic office application skills but have no prior programming or AI background. After completing the training, learners will be able to draft correspondence, process spreadsheet data, direct supervised AI agents, and maintain organizational confidentiality and compliance standards.
Target Audience
Business professionals, administrative assistants, operations personnel, and project coordinators seeking to integrate generative AI safely into daily office tasks.
Prerequisites
Basic familiarity with standard web browsers and office applications such as word processors and spreadsheets, with no prior AI or technical experience required.
Learning Outcomes

Course learning objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, you will be able to:

  • Identify core generative AI concepts, capabilities, limitations, and workplace compliance rules concerning data privacy and intellectual property.
  • Construct structured, role-based prompts using proven prompting frameworks across major conversational AI platforms.
  • Generate, summarize, and refine professional business documents and communications while applying effective human-in-the-loop review.
  • Apply AI-assisted prompts to spreadsheet tasks including formula creation, data cleaning, and tabular summary generation.
  • Direct browser-based autonomous AI agents through multi-step administrative workflows while mitigating hallucinations and quality risks.
