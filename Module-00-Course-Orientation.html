Course Vision & Structure

Comprehensive orientation to AI Fundamentals for Knowledge Workers. Covers the complete curriculum roadmap, target audience background, prerequisite verification, hands-on environment and tooling readiness checklist, and recommended learning strategies. 🎯 Course Learning Objectives

This course will cover the following objectives:

  • Identify core generative AI concepts, capabilities, limitations, and workplace compliance rules concerning data privacy and intellectual property.
  • Construct structured, role-based prompts using proven prompting frameworks across major conversational AI platforms.
  • Generate, summarize, and refine professional business documents and communications while applying effective human-in-the-loop review.
  • Apply AI-assisted prompts to spreadsheet tasks including formula creation, data cleaning, and tabular summary generation.
  • Direct browser-based autonomous AI agents through multi-step administrative workflows while mitigating hallucinations and quality risks.

Environment & Tooling Readiness

Hands-on Environment & Tooling

Practical application is central to this curriculum. To get the full value of the exercises and lab assignments, verify your tools and environment before starting Module 1.

Readiness Checklist

  • Web Browser Access: Ensure you have access to a modern desktop web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Apple Safari).
  • Generative AI Platform Account: Verify active access to at least one major conversational AI tool (such as free accounts on OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, or Microsoft Copilot).
  • Office Applications: Ensure access to standard word processing and spreadsheet applications (such as Microsoft 365, Google Docs/Sheets, or desktop equivalents) to test and review AI prompt outputs.
  • Data Governance Safeguards: Follow your organization's data protection policies: never input confidential company records, proprietary customer data, or unapproved personal data into public AI services.

💡 Key Takeaways

  • Verify terminal, software, and account credentials prior to Module 1.
  • Review the readiness checklist to ensure an unblocked lab experience.
  • Access sandbox environments and documentation links before starting practical work.
