## 📚 Course Overview & Curriculum Roadmap

**Duration:** 1 Day · 360 Minutes (6.0 Hours Core) + 30 Min Orientation · 120 Min Hands-On Labs  
**Level:** Beginner (No programming or prior AI background required)  
**Target Audience:** Business professionals, administrative assistants, operations personnel, and project coordinators.

### ⏱️ Course & Module Timing Breakdown

| Curriculum Component | Lessons & Demos | Hands-On Guided Lab | Review & Knowledge Check | Total Module Duration |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Module 0: Orientation (Optional)** | 25 min | — | 5 min | **30 min** |
| **Module 1: Foundations & Governance** | 50 min | **30 min** *(Evaluating Safe Data Inputs & Governance)* | 10 min | **90 min** |
| **Module 2: Prompt Engineering & Content** | 45 min | **30 min** *(Multi-Platform Structured Prompting)* | 10 min | **85 min** |
| **Module 3: Documents & Spreadsheets** | 50 min | **30 min** *(Document Drafting & Formulas)* | 10 min | **90 min** |
| **Module 4: Supervising AI Workflows** | 55 min | **30 min** *(Supervising Multi-Step Workflows)* | 10 min | **95 min** |
| **Core Curriculum Total (Modules 1–4)** | **200 min** | **120 min (2.0 Hours Labs)** | **40 min** | **360 min (6.0 Hours)** |
| **Complete Course with Orientation** | 225 min | 120 min | 45 min | **390 min (6.5 Hours)** |

### Module Breakdown & Hands-on Labs:

* **[Module 0: Course Orientation (Welcome & Roadmap)](Module-00-Course-Orientation.html)** *(30 min total)*  
  Course vision, complete curriculum roadmap, prerequisite verification, and browser/account readiness checklist.
* **[Module 1: AI Foundations, Responsible Use, and Workplace Governance](Module-01.html)** *(90 min total: 50m Lecture + 30m Lab + 10m Review)*  
  Understand core generative AI concepts, terminology, data privacy safeguards, IP boundaries, and organizational policies.  
  🧪 **Lab 1 (30 min):** *Evaluating Safe Data Inputs & Governance Verification*
* **[Module 2: Prompt Engineering and Business Content Generation](Module-02.html)** *(85 min total: 45m Lecture + 30m Lab + 10m Review)*  
  Master structured prompting frameworks (Role, Context, Task, Constraints), few-shot prompting, tone calibration, and iterative refinement.  
  🧪 **Lab 2 (30 min):** *Multi-Platform Structured Prompting & Comparative Iteration*
* **[Module 3: Streamlining Document Drafting and Spreadsheet Analysis](Module-03.html)** *(90 min total: 50m Lecture + 30m Lab + 10m Review)*  
  Draft executive correspondence, synthesize meeting notes, and execute spreadsheet formulas, data cleaning, and tabular summaries.  
  🧪 **Lab 3 (30 min):** *Document Drafting, Data Cleaning & Spreadsheet Formulas*
* **[Module 4: Supervising Task-Oriented AI Agents and Everyday Workflows](Module-04.html)** *(95 min total: 55m Lecture + 30m Lab + 10m Review)*  
  Direct multi-step browser-based workflows and simulated autonomous AI agents while establishing permission checkpoints and mitigating hallucinations.  
  🧪 **Lab 4 (30 min):** *Supervising Multi-Step Workflow Orchestration*
